UN SDG 3 — Good Health & Well-Being

What is SDG 3?

The United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 3 aims to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all people at all ages. It sets ambitious global health targets to be achieved by 2030 — covering physical health, mental health, and universal healthcare access.

Key Targets Related to Mental Health

Target 3.4

Reduce premature mortality from non-communicable diseases and promote mental health and well-being for all.

Target 3.8

Achieve universal health coverage with access to quality mental health services without financial hardship.

Target 3.5

Strengthen prevention and treatment of substance abuse and harmful alcohol use among young people.

Target 3.d

Strengthen capacity for early warning, risk reduction, and management of health threats at national level.

How MindBreath Hub Contributes

MindBreath Hub directly supports SDG Target 3.4 by providing college students with scientifically informed mental health assessment tools, evidence-based stress relief techniques, and direct connections to professional support networks — all in one accessible platform. Early identification through our Master Quiz helps reduce the burden of untreated mental health conditions.
