Practical De-stress Modalities

  • Abdominal Breathing: Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4, exhale for 6 to quiet active physiological alarms.
  • Kinetic Grounding: A rapid 20-minute physical walk alters core focus away from mental feedback loops.
  • Sensory De-escalation: Disconnect visual feeds 30 minutes before sleep to facilitate restorative delta waves.
